# Thummapala homepage and inner pages

## Scope
- Reshape the homepage into a shorter bilingual story with compact census figures, an About section, equal-size swipeable focus cards, and brief previews for the remaining topics.
- Add dedicated pages for Our Village, Education, Agrotech, Sports, Opportunities, Events, Activities, Stories, Gallery, and Contact.
- Connect navigation, homepage calls to action, and footer links to those pages; give every page distinct search and social metadata.
- Keep illustrative content clearly labeled, retain the bilingual experience and enquiry form, and do not imply that sample stories, events, opportunities, or photos are verified or transmitted.

## Technical approach
- Keep the project on TanStack Start and implement each page as a real route.
- Store bilingual page content in a browser-safe data module and share a consistent page layout.
- Reuse existing image pointers, accessible controls, and the current client-only enquiry behavior.
- Add focused tests for the required page set and focus-card destinations; verify tests and key desktop/mobile views.
